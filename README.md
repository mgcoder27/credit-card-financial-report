# Credit Card Analytics Dashboard

A two-page Power BI project analyzing 2023 credit card **transactions** and **customers** to show where revenue comes from, who drives it, and how to grow it.

- **Credit Card Transaction Report**: revenue, interest, amount and volume by card category, expenditure type, swipe method, education and job.
- **Credit Card Customer Report**: customer demographics (age, gender, marital status, dependents, education, salary, state) and their revenue contribution.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Track core KPIs: revenue, interest, transaction amount and transaction count.
- Find the best-performing card categories and spending types.
- Identify the customer segments that generate the most revenue.
- Understand quarterly and weekly revenue trends.

---

## 📊 Key Findings

**Overall**
- Total revenue is **57M**, interest earned is **8M**, transaction amount is **46M** across **656K** transactions.
- Total customer income is **588M** and the CSS score is **3.19**.

**Card Category**
- Blue cards generate **47M (~83%)** of revenue, far ahead of Silver (6M), Gold (3M) and Platinum (1M).

**Transaction Behaviour**
- Swipe leads with **36M**, followed by Chip (17M) and Online (4M).
- Bills (14M), Entertainment (10M) and Fuel (10M) are the top spending types; Travel is lowest at 6M.
- Revenue is stable across quarters (13.8M to 14.5M). Q4 has the highest revenue but the lowest transaction count (161.6K).

**Customer Segments**
- Businessmen contribute the most revenue (**18M**), followed by White-collar (10M); Retirees contribute the least (5M).
- Graduates lead by education (**23M**); Post-Graduates and Doctorates contribute only 2M to 3M.
- Males contribute **31M** and females **26M**.
- Customers aged **40 to 60** drive most of the revenue; ages 20 to 30 and 60+ contribute the least.
- High-income customers generate the most revenue (~30M); low-income customers contribute the least.
- Married customers (~29M) outperform single customers (~24M).
- Texas, New York and California are the top states at ~13M each, followed by Florida and New Jersey.

---

## ✅ Recommendations

1. **Upgrade Blue cardholders**: promote Silver, Gold and Platinum offers to high-spending Blue customers, since premium cards contribute under 10% of revenue.
2. **Grow online usage**: run cashback or reward campaigns on online payments, as it is the smallest channel (4M).
3. **Target top spending categories**: partner with bill-payment, entertainment and fuel merchants for rewards.
4. **Boost travel spending**: launch travel-specific offers, as it is the lowest category.
5. **Focus on high-value segments**: prioritize businessmen, graduates, high-income and 40 to 60 age customers for acquisition and retention.
6. **Engage low-income and younger customers**: use starter offers and limit-increase incentives to unlock growth.
7. **Investigate Q4**: revenue rose while transactions dropped, so check whether higher average transaction value is driving it and plan seasonal campaigns around it.
8. **Strengthen the top states**: invest in TX, NY and CA, and grow underperforming states such as NJ.
